# Code of Conduct

This applies to issues, pull requests, and other discussions in this repository.

- Be respectful. Critique ideas and products without personal attacks.
- Harassment, discrimination, threats, and sharing private information without permission are not acceptable.
- Keep discussions relevant. Follow the [contribution guide](contributing.md) when suggesting tools, and avoid spam or repeated promotion.

Report concerns privately to [hello@revmanic.com](mailto:hello@revmanic.com). The maintainer will review reports privately and may remove content or restrict participation when needed.
