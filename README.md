> Tools for B2B sales, RevOps and go-to-market (GTM) teams.

Browse tools for finding prospects, running outreach, closing deals and keeping customers. Each product appears once, under its main use. We look for a clear sales workflow and a useful difference from the tools already listed.

[Suggest a tool or correction](https://github.com/ilyaspiridonov/awesome-sales-tools/issues/new/choose), or read the [contribution guide](contributing.md).

## CRM

- [Attio](https://attio.com) - CRM with a flexible data model and email and calendar sync. Organize customer records around your sales process and automate follow-up sequences.
- [Close](https://close.com) - Sales CRM for small teams with built-in calling, SMS and email. Contact prospects and track the resulting conversations and deals in one place.
- [folk](https://www.folk.app) - Lightweight CRM with LinkedIn contact capture. Save prospects from LinkedIn, fill in missing contact details and send email campaigns.
- [HubSpot](https://www.hubspot.com) - CRM with a free tier and paid Sales, Marketing and Service hubs. Keep customer records, assign leads and track deals across teams.
- [Pipedrive](https://www.pipedrive.com) - CRM built around a visual sales pipeline. Move deals through stages, record sales activities and forecast revenue.
- [Salesforce Sales Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/cloud/) - CRM with configurable sales workflows and territory management. Track accounts and deals, automate approvals and tailor forecasts to your sales process.
- [Zoho CRM](https://www.zoho.com/crm/) - CRM in the Zoho suite with Blueprint process controls on paid tiers. Track deals and define the steps and required information for each sales stage.

## B2B Data and Sales Intelligence

- [Apollo.io](https://www.apollo.io) - Contact and company database with built-in sequences and calling. Build prospect lists and start email or phone outreach from the same tool.
- [BuiltWith](https://builtwith.com) - Database of the technologies used on websites. Find companies using particular software and build lists for prospecting.
- [Cognism](https://www.cognism.com) - Contact and company database with mobile numbers and EMEA coverage. Find business contacts and add phone numbers and company details to existing records.
- [Crunchbase](https://www.crunchbase.com) - Private-company database covering funding, leadership and company characteristics. Research target accounts and spot funding rounds or leadership changes that give you a reason to reach out.
- [Kaspr](https://www.kaspr.io) - LinkedIn browser extension from Cognism. Look up contact details while browsing profiles and add them to prospect records.
- [LinkedIn Sales Navigator](https://business.linkedin.com/sales-solutions/sales-navigator) - LinkedIn's lead and account search tool with alerts and InMail. Find relevant decision-makers, follow account updates and contact prospects on LinkedIn.
- [Lusha](https://www.lusha.com) - Contact and company data available through an extension, CSV upload, or API. Look up business contacts and filter prospects by research activity on relevant topics.
- [ZoomInfo](https://www.zoominfo.com) - Company and contact database with intent signals and website visitor identification. Build account lists, find contacts and prioritize companies showing relevant research or website activity.

## Data Enrichment and Contact Validation

- [Clay](https://www.clay.com) - Spreadsheet-style workflow builder combining data providers and AI research. Fill gaps in prospect lists and pass the results between sales tools.
- [Dropcontact](https://www.dropcontact.com) - Email finder and data cleaner for files, Google Sheets, CRMs and API use. Find business email addresses, check their validity and merge duplicate records.
- [FullEnrich](https://fullenrich.com) - Contact enrichment that tries multiple data providers in sequence. Find and verify contact details when a single provider leaves gaps.
- [Hunter](https://hunter.io) - Email search and verification with outreach campaigns. Find addresses by person or company domain, check them and send follow-up emails.
- [NeverBounce](https://neverbounce.com) - Email verifier with bulk uploads, a form widget and an API. Check lists before sending or validate addresses as people submit them.

## Buyer Signals and Intent Data

Tools for spotting relevant activity and deciding which accounts to approach; a signal alone does not establish buying intent.

- [6sense](https://6sense.com) - Account intelligence and advertising for B2B sales and marketing. Find companies researching relevant topics and prioritize accounts by fit and buying signals.
- [Bombora](https://bombora.com) - Intent data drawn from research activity across a publisher network. Use topic interest to prioritize accounts in your CRM and advertising campaigns.
- [Common Room](https://www.commonroom.io) - Combined view of product usage, website visits, community activity and job changes. Connect these signals to contacts and accounts, prioritize them and assign follow-up.
- [Demandbase One](https://www.demandbase.com) - Account intelligence with intent data and advertising. Find target accounts, map the people involved in a purchase and track their research interests.
- [RB2B](https://www.rb2b.com) - Website visitor identification with person-level matching limited to US visitors. See which visitors can be matched to professional profiles for follow-up.
- [UserGems](https://www.usergems.com) - Job-change tracking for past customers and contacts. Find new sales opportunities when people who know your product move to another company.
- [Warmly](https://warmly.ai) - Website visitor identification with live chat and lead routing. Use available visitor matches and browsing activity to start conversations or assign follow-up to a rep.

## Scheduling and Lead Routing

- [Cal.com](https://cal.com) - Scheduling software with booking pages, team routing and an API. Let prospects choose a meeting time and book with the appropriate team member.
- [Calendly](https://calendly.com) - Scheduling software with routing forms and meeting polls. Share your availability, match visitors to a rep, or poll a group for a suitable time.
- [Chili Piper](https://www.chilipiper.com) - Lead routing and scheduling for web forms and chat. Assign inbound leads and let qualified prospects book a meeting as they get in touch.
- [LeanData](https://www.leandata.com) - Salesforce-native lead matching and routing with BookIt scheduling. Match leads to accounts, assign ownership and book meetings with the right rep.
- [SavvyCal](https://savvycal.com) - Scheduling links with a calendar overlay for recipients. Let guests compare your available times against their own calendar before booking.

## Sales Engagement

Workspaces for salespeople to plan and run outreach across several channels.

- [Amplemarket](https://www.amplemarket.com) - Outbound sales software combining contact data, buying signals and deliverability tools. Find prospects and plan follow-up across multiple channels using their recent activity.
- [Outreach](https://www.outreach.ai) - Sales engagement software with calling, deal management and forecasting. Organize rep follow-up, run email sequences and track deals through to the revenue forecast.
- [Reply.io](https://reply.io) - Outreach sequences across email, LinkedIn, calls, SMS and WhatsApp, with an optional AI SDR. Find prospects and combine several channels in a meeting-booking campaign.
- [Salesforge](https://www.salesforge.ai) - Email and LinkedIn outreach with multi-mailbox sending. Run follow-up sequences and handle replies from a shared inbox.
- [Salesloft](https://www.salesloft.com) - Sales engagement software with conversation analysis and deal management. Plan daily outreach, make calls and review team conversations for coaching.

## AI SDR and Sales Agents

Tools that automate parts of prospect research, outreach, or reply handling; the work left to a person varies by product.

- [11x](https://www.11x.ai) - AI agents for outbound prospecting and inbound lead qualification. Automate prospect research, outreach and meeting booking, then hand conversations to sales reps.
- [Artisan](https://www.artisan.co) - AI prospecting agent called Ava, alongside a dialer for sales reps. Automate prospect research and outreach with Ava and make calls yourself through the dialer.
- [Oliv](https://www.oliv.ai) - AI agents for prospecting, deal follow-up and customer account management. Research prospects, prepare CRM updates and flag renewal risks or expansion opportunities.
- [Regie.ai](https://regie.ai) - Prospecting workspace combining AI agents, a CRM and a dialer. Find target accounts and combine automated outreach with rep-led calls.

## Cold Email Platforms

Tools centered on sending email campaigns and managing replies, with other channels available in some products.

- [Instantly](https://instantly.ai) - Cold email platform with a lead database, warm-up and purchasable domains and mailboxes. Set up sending accounts, run campaigns and monitor deliverability in one place.
- [lemlist](https://www.lemlist.com) - Email, LinkedIn and calling campaigns with a lead database and warm-up. Find prospects and combine email follow-ups with social messages and calls.
- [Smartlead](https://www.smartlead.ai) - Cold email platform with mailbox rotation and warm-up. Distribute campaign sending across accounts and handle replies in a unified inbox.

## Email Deliverability and Sending Infrastructure

- [EasyDMARC](https://easydmarc.com) - Email authentication monitoring for DMARC, SPF and DKIM. Check domain configuration and work through the steps to enforce a DMARC policy.
- [GlockApps](https://glockapps.com) - Inbox placement testing across mailbox providers. Send test messages to see whether they reach the inbox or spam folder.
- [Google Postmaster Tools](https://postmaster.google.com) - Free dashboards for domains sending email to Gmail accounts. Check domain statistics and compliance with Google's sender requirements.
- [Maildoso](https://maildoso.ai) - Outbound email infrastructure with automated DNS setup and warm-up. Set up sending domains and mailboxes and monitor their deliverability.
- [MailReach](https://www.mailreach.co) - Mailbox warm-up service with spam testing. Run automated warm-up activity and test whether messages reach the inbox.
- [Mailreef](https://www.mailreef.com) - Managed email infrastructure on dedicated servers and IPs. Set up outbound domains and mailboxes on infrastructure reserved for your sending.
- [MxToolbox](https://mxtoolbox.com) - Email and DNS diagnostics with paid monitoring. Investigate blocklist entries, domain configuration and mail delivery problems.

## Sales Dialers and Cloud Telephony

- [Aircall](https://aircall.io) - Cloud phone system with call menus, recording and SMS. Give sales teams phone numbers, route incoming calls and record conversations for review.
- [CloudTalk](https://www.cloudtalk.io) - Cloud phone system with international numbers and outbound dialers. Call through prospect lists and manage conversations across countries.
- [Dialpad](https://www.dialpad.com) - Cloud calling, messaging and meetings with built-in transcription. Speak with prospects and review transcripts to understand what happens on team calls.
- [JustCall](https://justcall.io) - Cloud phone and SMS system with outbound dialers and call scoring. Contact prospects, record conversations and use call scores to guide coaching.
- [Nooks](https://www.nooks.ai) - Parallel dialer with answer detection and a shared virtual salesfloor. Call prospects alongside teammates and coach reps during calling sessions.
- [Orum](https://www.orum.com) - Parallel and power dialer with voicemail automation and a call library. Work through call lists, leave recorded voicemails and review calls for coaching.

## Social Selling and LinkedIn Automation

- [Expandi](https://expandi.io) - Cloud-based LinkedIn automation with email outreach. Schedule connection requests, messages and InMails as part of a follow-up sequence.
- [HeyReach](https://www.heyreach.io) - LinkedIn outreach automation across multiple sender accounts. Run campaigns for several senders and manage replies in one inbox.
- [La Growth Machine](https://lagrowthmachine.com) - Prospecting across LinkedIn, email, X and calls, including LinkedIn voice messages. Add missing prospect data and combine channel-specific steps in a follow-up sequence.

## Video Prospecting and Gifting

- [Loom](https://www.loom.com) - Screen and camera recorder with transcripts and shareable links. Record a walkthrough or personal introduction that a prospect can watch in their own time.
- [Sendoso](https://www.sendoso.com) - Gifting platform with inventory storage and fulfillment for physical and digital gifts. Send gifts or direct mail and follow delivery progress.
- [Sendspark](https://www.sendspark.com) - Video personalization using AI voice cloning and dynamic backgrounds. Adapt a recording for individual recipients and track how they engage with it.
- [Vidyard](https://www.vidyard.com) - Video messaging and hosting with screen recording and AI avatars. Send personalized sales videos and receive notifications when prospects watch them.

## Conversation Intelligence and Meeting Assistants

- [Avoma](https://www.avoma.com) - Meeting assistant with scheduling and revenue forecasting. Capture notes and follow-ups and review calls to coach sales reps.
- [Chorus](https://www.zoominfo.com/products/chorus) - Sales call recording and analysis from ZoomInfo. Review conversations and compare patterns across the team's calls.
- [Fathom](https://www.fathom.ai) - Meeting recorder with CRM sync. Turn calls into transcripts and summaries and review them for coaching.
- [Gong](https://www.gong.io) - Sales call recording with deal health and coaching tools. Review conversations to coach reps, assess deals and forecast revenue.
- [Otter.ai](https://otter.ai) - Transcription for live meetings and imported audio. Summarize discussions and ask questions about them through AI chat.
- [tl;dv](https://tldv.io) - Meeting recorder with a bot-free recording option. Generate meeting minutes, update CRM records and review calls for coaching.

## Demo Platforms

- [Arcade](https://www.arcade.software) - Demo builder for software walkthroughs. Turn captures into interactive demos or AI videos, share them and track engagement.
- [Consensus](https://goconsensus.com) - Interactive and video demos for buyers to explore on their own. Share product demonstrations and see how buyers engage with them.
- [Navattic](https://www.navattic.com) - Interactive product demos for websites and sales teams. Identify demo visitors and track how they explore the product.
- [Reprise](https://www.reprise.com) - Demo platform for enterprise teams. Build guided walkthroughs, live demo environments and application clones for sales presentations.
- [Storylane](https://www.storylane.io) - Interactive demo builder with buyer-facing content hubs. Capture screenshots, video, or HTML to build demos, collect leads and track engagement.

## Digital Sales Rooms

- [Aligned](https://alignedup.com) - Deal rooms with stakeholder mapping and shared milestones. Share resources, track buyer participation and agree on next steps with the buying team.
- [Arrows](https://arrows.to) - Sales rooms with AI agents that prepare updates from calls and CRM records. Review drafted follow-ups, CRM changes and room updates before approving them.
- [Dock](https://www.dock.us) - Buyer workspaces connected to a shared sales content library. Reuse your team's materials, build mutual action plans and send signable order forms.
- [GetAccept](https://www.getaccept.com) - Buyer workspaces combining content, proposals and contracts. Send proposals, track engagement and collect signatures in one place.
- [HummingDeck](https://hummingdeck.com) - Digital sales rooms with page-by-page document tracking. Share content, track engagement and coordinate a mutual action plan.
- [Journey](https://journey.io) - Single-page buyer workspaces. Package your pitch, customer evidence, pricing and documents into one link and track engagement.

## Proposals and CPQ

- [DealHub](https://dealhub.io) - Quoting software with deal rooms and billing. Configure quotes, get commercial terms approved and collaborate on contracts.
- [PandaDoc](https://www.pandadoc.com) - Document workflows for proposals, quotes and contracts. Create documents from templates, track views, collect signatures and accept payments.
- [Qwilr](https://qwilr.com) - Proposals and quotes presented as web pages. Track buyer engagement, collect signatures and take payments.
- [Salesforce Revenue Cloud](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/revenue-lifecycle-management/revenue-cloud/) - Product catalog, quoting and billing inside Salesforce. Price deals, manage contracts and renewals and process orders and payments.

## Contracts and E-Signature

- [Adobe Acrobat Sign](https://www.adobe.com/acrobat/business/sign.html) - Electronic signature software with bulk sending and audit trails. Collect signatures through reusable templates and web forms.
- [DocuSign](https://www.docusign.com) - Electronic signature and agreement management with a document repository. Negotiate agreements, request signatures and collect payments.
- [Dropbox Sign](https://sign.dropbox.com) - Electronic signature software and API, formerly HelloSign. Request signatures using templates or embed signing in your own app.
- [Ironclad](https://ironcladapp.com) - Contract management with a shared repository. Draft and redline agreements, route them for approval and collect signatures.
- [Juro](https://juro.com) - Browser-based contract management with AI review. Create contracts from templates, negotiate terms and collect signatures.
- [SpotDraft](https://www.spotdraft.com) - Contract management with legal intake and AI review. Handle contract requests, redline agreements and collect signatures.

## Trust and Security Review

- [Conveyor](https://www.conveyor.com) - Trust center and security questionnaire software. Draft answers from approved company knowledge and share security information with buyers.
- [Loopio](https://loopio.com) - Shared answer library with AI drafting for response teams. Reuse answers in RFPs, security questionnaires and due-diligence requests.
- [Responsive](https://www.responsive.io) - Response management with a trust center, formerly RFPIO. Coordinate responses to RFPs, RFIs and security questionnaires.
- [Vanta](https://www.vanta.com) - Compliance automation with a customer-facing trust center. Share security evidence with buyers and automate questionnaire responses.
- [Whistic](https://www.whistic.com) - Vendor risk assessment software with trust centers. Assess vendors and share your own security information with buyers through trust centers and questionnaires.

## Customer Onboarding and Success

- [ChurnZero](https://churnzero.com) - Customer success software with health scores and automated playbooks. Track account health, manage renewals and find opportunities to expand accounts.
- [Gainsight](https://www.gainsight.com) - Customer success suite with success plans, playbooks and communities. Track customer health and plan renewals and account growth.
- [GUIDEcx](https://www.guidecx.com) - Shared onboarding workspaces for teams and their customers. Build plans from templates, track milestones and pass customer details between teams.
- [Planhat](https://www.planhat.com) - Customer success, CRM and professional services tools built on shared customer data. Monitor account health and plan renewals and expansion.
- [Rocketlane](https://www.rocketlane.com) - Customer onboarding and professional services software. Plan resources, coordinate work through customer portals and carry customer context between teams.

## Customer Evidence, Advocacy and Referrals

- [Influitive](https://influitive.com) - Customer advocacy communities with rewards programs. Invite customers to make referrals and share their experiences.
- [Referral Rock](https://referralrock.com) - Referral, ambassador and affiliate program software. Give participants a portal, track referrals and manage rewards.
- [Senja](https://senja.io) - Text and video testimonial collection with embeddable displays. Gather customer feedback through forms and publish it as widgets or testimonial walls.
- [UserEvidence](https://userevidence.com) - Customer evidence software using surveys and sales calls. Turn customer feedback into testimonials, ROI statistics and proof pages for sellers to share.

## Forecasting and Revenue Analytics

- [Aviso](https://www.aviso.com) - Revenue forecasting with conversation analysis and deal guidance. Use CRM and sales activity data to assess deals and forecast the pipeline.
- [Backstory](https://www.backstory.ai) - Sales activity capture and engagement dashboards, formerly People.ai. Sync activity to the CRM and use it to review pipeline performance and forecasts.
- [Dreamdata](https://dreamdata.io) - B2B revenue attribution connecting marketing and sales data. Trace pipeline and revenue back to the activities that contributed to them.
- [HockeyStack](https://www.hockeystack.com) - Buyer journey analytics combining website, marketing, sales and CRM data. Review pipeline performance and connect marketing activities to revenue.
- [Ruler Analytics](https://www.ruleranalytics.com) - Revenue attribution across website visits and offline interactions. Connect those interactions to leads, opportunities and revenue.

## Sales Enablement and Training

- [Allego](https://www.allego.com) - Sales training and coaching with call recording and digital sales rooms. Onboard reps, review conversations and organize the content they share with buyers.
- [Guru](https://www.getguru.com) - Internal knowledge base with content verification workflows. Maintain playbooks and find answers through a browser extension, Slack, or Teams.
- [Highspot](https://www.highspot.com) - Sales content and training software, merged with Seismic in 2026. Organize materials, see how buyers engage with them and coach reps.
- [Hyperbound](https://www.hyperbound.ai) - AI buyer simulations for sales role-play. Practice conversations and use scores and feedback to guide coaching.
- [Mindtickle](https://www.mindtickle.com) - Sales training with certifications, AI role-play and conversation analysis. Onboard reps and combine practice sessions with coaching from real calls.
- [Showpad](https://www.showpad.com) - Sales content library with buyer-facing Shared Spaces. Organize materials, share them with buyers and track engagement to inform follow-ups.
- [Spekit](https://www.spekit.com) - Knowledge and training tools embedded in everyday work. Organize sales content and give reps contextual guidance and learning paths.

## Incentive Compensation and Sales Planning

- [CaptivateIQ](https://www.captivateiq.com) - Commission automation with sales planning tools. Calculate commissions, plan territories and set quotas.
- [Everstage](https://www.everstage.com) - Commission management with earnings forecasts and dispute handling. Design plans, calculate payouts and resolve questions about earnings.
- [QuotaPath](https://www.quotapath.com) - Commission tracking connected to CRM, accounting and payroll systems. Bring sales and payment data together to track earnings against targets.
- [Salesforce Spiff](https://www.salesforce.com/sales/incentive-compensation-management/) - Commission management inside Salesforce. Design incentive plans and automate commission calculations.
- [Varicent](https://www.varicent.com) - Enterprise sales planning and compensation software. Model territories, quotas and sales capacity alongside commission plans.

## Workflow Automation and CRM Data Operations

- [Cloudingo](https://cloudingo.com) - Data cleanup for Salesforce. Find and merge duplicates, standardize records and update accounts and contacts in bulk.
- [Hightouch](https://hightouch.com) - Data syncing from a warehouse to CRM and advertising tools. Build audiences from warehouse data and keep destination systems up to date.
- [Insycle](https://www.insycle.com) - CRM data maintenance with recurring workflows. Remove duplicates, standardize records and automate lead and account routing.
- [Make](https://www.make.com) - Visual workflow builder with branching, filters and data stores. Connect apps and automate how data moves between them.
- [n8n](https://n8n.io) - Source-available workflow automation ([source](https://github.com/n8n-io/n8n)) with self-hosting. Connect apps through a visual builder and add code steps for custom logic.
- [Workato](https://www.workato.com) - Enterprise integration software with API management and governance. Connect applications and automate shared workflows using reusable recipes.
- [Zapier](https://zapier.com) - App automation with tables, interfaces and agents. Connect triggers and actions to route leads and keep records in sync across apps.
